# ADR-0005: this book absorbs and retires the author's earlier books

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## Context

The author wrote earlier books on Spec-Driven Development, the FOCUS architecture and context engineering.
The FOCUS book explained the architecture poorly and grew much longer than it should.
Those manuscripts are private and unlicensed.

## Decision

This book is self-contained and replaces them.
It does not mention them, and presents none as a prerequisite or a complement.
Fundamentals are rewritten from primary sources, never copied from those manuscripts.
FOCUS is redefined and simplified here.

## Consequences

The book must teach context and SDD fundamentals itself, briefly.
focus-kit's references to the earlier FOCUS book are repointed at launch (docs/00 OD-4).
