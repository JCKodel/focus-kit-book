# ADR-0015: chapters open with their value and carry no filler

**Date:** 2026-09-24

## Context

A reader who tires drops the method, and this book is not commercial material that needs length.
A length target does harm both ways: it invites filler in a short chapter and cuts useful content from a long one.

## Decision

Every chapter opens with what the reader can do after it, in at most three sentences.
It is as long as proving that value takes: no filler added, no useful content removed.
There is no word or reading-time target.

## Consequences

The product questions of docs/00 ask whether every sentence carries value and whether anything the reader needs is missing; every milestone review asks both.
