# ch02-gain-less-to-read

Draft, from the author's reading of the book on 2026-10-18.

* Chapter 2's "What the team gains" does not make explicit that focus-kit's gain is writing less, because the person *has* to read what is written, and the less there is to read, the more attention each part gets.
* This is one of the process's main benefits; the book describes it well but never says it outright.
* Write for a reader who will not infer it: say it plainly.
