# early-no-forward-links

Draft, from the author's reading of the book on 2026-10-18.

* The first chapters are full of links to future, distant chapters. The reader reads the whole book and will remember a short introduction; they do not need to know where an idea lives.
* Where a chapter names an idea taught later, it explains the idea in a few words, there, and drops the pointer.
* Exception, said by the author: the gain sections ("What the team gains") keep their explanation of the parts and their pointers; they are useful.
* Scope chosen by the author: chapters 0 to 7, both editions.
