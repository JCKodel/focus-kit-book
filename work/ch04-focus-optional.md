# ch04-focus-optional

Draft, from the author's reading of the book on 2026-10-18.

* Chapter 4 opens with a disclaimer: FOCUS gathers the best qualities of the best practices, distilled into a simple, effective architecture, but it is optional.
* focus-kit asks whether to create a new project with FOCUS or migrate an existing one; it is completely optional, and the team should first understand the architecture and the value it delivers, and whether it makes sense.
* Encourage the reader to read, above all "What the team gains", and decide whether the team sees the value.
* The gain adds, as the author decided: when a company uses the same pattern in every project, moving people between teams costs less. Say it is the author's reasoning if no measured number backs it (docs/04).
