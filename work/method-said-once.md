# method-said-once

**Objective.** A reader of chapters 9 to 19 meets each rule of the method, each list of marks, each number and each story once, in the chapter that owns it, and the passages that read as the kit's manual are cut to what the reader uses; both editions.

**Decided from the documents.**
1. The owner of a passage is its first concrete occurrence (AGENTS.md, "abstraction on the second concrete occurrence"); a later chapter keeps one sentence and the link, as chapters already link each other (docs/04, Context before an excerpt: a reader who has read the previous chapters understands what is shown).
2. "Reads as the kit's manual" means a passage that lists what a command reads, marks or prints, in the order of the command's file, with no error it prevents and no decision the reader takes (docs/04, Teach, do not report; docs/00's reader, who runs the kit and does not maintain it).
3. Nothing a chapter teaches for the first time leaves; the cuts below are only repetitions, and each names where the passage stays (docs/04, No filler: never cut useful content).
4. Numbers: a measured number is given once, where it proves its point; a gain section keeps the one number that sums its chapter (docs/04, Numbers that prove).
5. This page lists the cuts by section name, not by line, since M14's chapter lines land before it and move the lines; /apply finds each passage by the words quoted.
6. Chapters 13, 14, 15, 17 and 19 are also touched by M14 (`ch17-review-reads-code`, `ch15-24-whole-queue`, `ch13-14-19-line-stops`, `ch14-18-kit-wording`); this delivery runs after `m14-review`, and cuts nothing those lines add.
