# ch11-update-2026-10-16

**Objective.** After chapter 11 a reader can read the report of an update to focus-kit 2026.10.16: besides a rule in docs/04, docs/05 or `AGENTS.md`, it names the docs/06 lines that carry history and the docs/05 §5 slots that narrate steps or output, each with the shorter wording, and the reader knows a version can pass with no migration at all.

**Behaviour.**

* A reader on the lending library updating from 2026.10.13 to 2026.10.16 expects a report that names, for each migration, the path, the section or line and the proposed wording: docs/05 §2 (a request may continue as `/propose` in the same session), §4 (a queue line is the mark, the slug, one sentence and its suffixes), §5 (a slot says what and the command; the Manuals slot also holds a proof `/apply` runs on another machine), `AGENTS.md`'s "One delivery" line, a docs/06 line and a slot.
* A reader whose `members` line in docs/06 reads "the librarian registers, suspends and reinstates members; suspended members keep their loans, as the librarian decided" sees the report propose `the librarian registers, suspends and reinstates members`, with the rest moved to `work/done/members.md` when that page lacks it; the update writes neither file.
* A reader whose Environments slot holds the steps to publish the app, written out, sees the report propose the slot's command and a pointer to `docs/manuals/publish-the-app.md`, as chapter 10 shows it.
* A reader asked "does the update shorten my queue for me?" answers no: docs/06 belongs to the project like docs/05, and the shortening is applied as a delivery, a queue line through `/propose` and `/apply`, as for any migration.
* Edge: a reader updating from 2026.10.13 to 2026.10.16 passes 2026.10.15, whose changelog entry says "No migration": it changed how a worktree per delivery is laid out, and a docs/05 whose Git slot names nothing already means the new default, so the report holds nothing from it.
* Edge: 2026.10.08.2 still reports no migration, since it changed only the commands.
* A reader who opens the changelog to know why the queue migration exists finds 2026.10.16's entry: in one project every `/apply` read about 180KB of documents before its first edit, with queue lines up to 4.7KB.
* Failure: no sentence in chapter 11 says an update touches only docs/04, docs/05 and `AGENTS.md` among the project's files, or that the update writes docs/06.
