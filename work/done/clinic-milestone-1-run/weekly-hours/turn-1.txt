I've read docs/00 to 06, ADR-0005 and the `professionals` page. Nothing is in `work/` yet. The git strategy is trunk, so the page will be written on `main` and I won't create a branch.

**My assessment.** This fits on one page. It adds one table (`working_period`, already listed in docs/02), two owner routes (read and save), one editor in each professional's row on the owner screen, and one pure use case that validates a whole week. The open decision about future appointments when hours change doesn't come up yet because no appointment exists. The current default ("existing appointments stay") keeps applying, and it will matter once `book-appointment` ships. Slots, and what happens around daylight-saving changes, belong to `book-appointment`.

Here is my plan, with a recommendation at the top of each question:

1. **How hours are saved.** I recommend editing the whole week and saving it with one "Save hours" button, which the server stores in one step (`PUT .../hours` with the full list of periods). The alternative is saving each period on its own as it is added or removed. Saving the week at once keeps the overlap check in one place, and the owner never ends up with half a week saved.

2. **Several periods on one day.** I recommend allowing them, for example 09:00 to 13:00 and 14:00 to 18:00 for a lunch break. docs/03 already assumes this ("periods of one professional on one weekday do not overlap"). Two periods that meet exactly (one ends at 13:00, the next starts at 13:00) don't count as overlapping.

3. **Which times are allowed.** I recommend HH:MM from 00:00 to 23:55, in steps of 5 minutes (the same step as the appointment length), with the start before the end. No period can cross midnight.

4. **A period too short for one appointment** (for example 09:00 to 09:20 when appointments are 30 minutes). I recommend refusing it, with a message that says the minimum. Otherwise the owner thinks they opened time that clients will never see. A period that isn't an exact multiple of the length (for example 09:00 to 12:10) is accepted, and the last 10 minutes simply hold no appointment.

5. **Where it appears.** I recommend an "Hours" button in each row, next to "Rename" and "Remove". It opens an editor under the row, and only one row is open at a time, the same rule as today. The editor lists the seven days from Monday to Sunday. Each day shows its periods (a "From" field, a "To" field and a "Remove period" button) and an "Add period" button. "Add period" fills in 09:00 to 17:00. At the bottom are "Save hours" and "Cancel".

6. **A summary in the list.** I recommend not showing a summary of each professional's hours in the row for now. The owner opens "Hours" to see them. The one exception I'd make is a short "No hours yet" next to a professional without periods, because clients won't be able to book that person. Do you want that line, or nothing at all?

7. **New names in docs/03.** I'd add three refusals, all pure and all with tests:
   - `InvalidWorkingPeriod`: a time in the wrong format or off the 5-minute step, or an end not after the start.
   - `WorkingPeriodTooShort`: the case in question 4.
   - `WorkingPeriodsOverlap`: two periods on the same day overlap.

   The server answers with the usual `{ "error": { "code" } }`. The screen runs the same use case before sending, only to show which period is wrong.
