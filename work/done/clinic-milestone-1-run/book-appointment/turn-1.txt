## Assessment

This fits one page, but only just. It's the biggest delivery so far because four things arrive together:
- slots worked out from the weekly hours in clinic time;
- the `appointment` table, with the index that stops double booking;
- the booking itself;
- the booking code, and what the phone remembers.

The list of remembered appointments with a cancel button belongs to `cancel-appointment`. Here I'd only save the appointment on the phone and show it (see question 2).

Planned contract:
- **`GET /api/professionals/:id/slots`:** public; answers with the start instants (UTC) of the free slots.
- **`POST /api/appointments`:** takes `{ professionalId, startsAt, clientName, clientPhone }` and answers `201` with the booking code.
- **Refusals:** `SlotTaken` (409), `OutsideWorkingHours`, `OutsideBookingWindow` and `ProfessionalNotFound`.
- **New refusals:** `InvalidClientName` and `InvalidPhoneNumber`. They go into docs/03 first.

## Questions (my recommendation first in each)

1. **Phone number.** I recommend accepting spaces, `+`, `-`, `.` and brackets, then requiring 6 to 15 digits (15 is the international maximum). The database would store **digits only**, which matches invariant 6. The downside: `+351 912…` and `912…` become different numbers, so the client must type it the same way to cancel. Alternatively we keep what they typed for the owner to read, plus a digits-only column for lookups. That's one more column, which pays off only once `owner-schedule` exists.

2. **The phone remembers the appointment.** I recommend saving it now (booking code, phone, professional, start) and showing a read-only "Your appointments" list on the home page, upcoming only. `cancel-appointment` would then add the button. The other option is to only save it now and show nothing until `cancel-appointment`. That's simpler, but step 4 of docs/00 couldn't be checked from the screen.

3. **Booking less than 24 hours ahead.** A slot 3 hours away can be booked but never cancelled in the app. I recommend a line on the confirmation step before booking: "This appointment starts in less than 24 hours and cannot be cancelled in the app." Alternatively we say nothing, and the client finds out in `cancel-appointment`.

4. **A professional with no free slots** (no hours, or everything booked). I recommend keeping them in the list, as `GET /api/professionals` does today, and showing "No free times in the next 30 days." after they're picked. This doesn't change any existing contract. Hiding them would need a new route or a change to that one.

5. **Choosing a time.** I recommend two steps: first the days that have at least one free slot ("Tue 29 Sep"), then that day's times ("09:30", 24-hour clock, the same format as the hours editor). The alternative is one long list of 30 days, which is poor on a phone.

6. **Fake bookings** (an open decision in docs/00). I recommend leaving it open in this delivery. The app only runs locally until `deploy`, and when that happens it becomes a question like `sign-in-limit`. Do you confirm that it stays open, or do you want a line in the queue before `deploy`?

## Choices I'll make unless you object

- **Booking window:** a slot's start is after now and at most now + 30×24h, exactly as invariant 3 says. So the last day may be partly cut off.
- **Clock changes:**
  - Slots follow the clock on the wall.
  - A time that doesn't exist on the spring-forward day is skipped.
  - A time that happens twice in autumn uses its first occurrence.
  - Overlaps are compared as instants.
  - No date library: Node's built-in `Intl` is enough.
- **Booking code:**
  - 6 characters from `ABCDEFGHJKMNPQRSTUVWXYZ23456789`, which leaves out the look-alikes 0, O, 1, I and L.
  - Each code is unique in the database, and a new one is drawn on the rare clash.
  - The random value is passed into the use case, so the rule stays testable.
- **Double booking:** the final guard is the partial unique index on `(professional_id, starts_at) WHERE status = 'booked'`. Losing a race answers `409 SlotTaken`, and the screen says "Someone just took this time. Pick another." and reloads the times.
- **Client name:** trimmed, 1 to 80 characters, reusing `lib/name.ts`.
- **Hours changed after booking:** the appointment stays, which is the current default in docs/00. Its time is simply no longer offered.
- **Nothing is written to the owner screen:** that's `owner-schedule`.
