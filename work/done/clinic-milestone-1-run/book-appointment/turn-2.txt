## What I chose and why

1. **Phone numbers are stored as digits only, 6 to 15 of them.** This matches invariant 6 (phones are compared by digits) and needs no extra column. The catch is that `+351 912…` and `912…` count as different numbers. To soften that, the booked screen shows the exact digits the client will need later.
2. **The phone saves the appointment, and the home page lists upcoming ones read-only under "Your appointments".** Without that, step 4 of docs/00 couldn't be checked on screen. The cancel button comes in `cancel-appointment`.
3. **A warning appears when the time is less than 24 hours away**, because otherwise the client learns they can't cancel only when they try. The check uses a `cancellationDeadline` rule written now, which `cancel-appointment` will then enforce.
4. **Professionals with no free times stay in the list** and say "No free times in the next 30 days." The public list of professionals doesn't change.
5. **Booking takes two steps: pick a day, then a time.** Thirty days of times in one list doesn't work on a phone.
6. **Fake bookings stay an open decision.** The app only runs locally until `deploy`, and the new `fake-bookings` line in the queue makes sure it gets decided before anyone outside can use it. It's one line in the queue, not a decision: undo it if you'd rather not.

## Choices on the page that deserve your eye

- **Three refusals share status `409`:**
  - `SlotTaken`
  - `OutsideWorkingHours`
  - `OutsideBookingWindow`

  The client shows the same message for all three ("This time is no longer free."). The error body still carries the exact code.
- **Rare booking-code clash:** it answers `500`, and the client's "Try again" draws a new code. I chose that over a retry loop. It should happen about once in 887 million bookings per existing appointment.
- **Clock changes:**
  - Slots follow the clock on the wall.
  - A time skipped when clocks go forward gives no slot.
  - A time that happens twice when clocks go back uses its first occurrence.
- **No date library:** time zone conversions use JavaScript's built-in `Intl`.
- **Booking window:** a slot must start after now and at most exactly 30×24 hours from now, so the last day may be cut short.
- **New code:**
  - It goes in a new folder, `src/features/appointments/`, which `cancel-appointment` will add to.
  - A new migration, `0004-appointment.sql`, creates the appointments table.
