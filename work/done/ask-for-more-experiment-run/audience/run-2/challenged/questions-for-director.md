**To:** Oriel Ashdown, Director of the Library
**Cc:** Administration and Finance Officer
**Subject:** Two questions on the annual report before the 19 February Board meeting

Dear Oriel,

Two points in the annual report will probably come up when the Board considers the budget. It would help to have answers before the meeting, so the 10 minutes can go on the decisions.

1. **2026 adult fee income (Section 10.2, Table 29).** The budget takes 5,300 adult members at 22 cr, which gives 116,600 cr. The increase applies only to memberships joined or renewed on or after 1 April, so members who renew in January–March would pay 20 cr until 2027. If renewals are spread evenly through the year, income would be about 2,650 cr lower, and the reserves headroom in Table 30 would fall from 5,968 cr to about 3,300 cr. Does the estimate already allow for this?

2. **Fines outstanding (Section 7.7, Table 20).** Fines of 580 cr were outstanding at 31 December 2024. In 2025, the outstanding figure of 800 cr equals the year's fines charged, less collected, less waived. So the 580 cr from 2024 does not appear as collected, waived or written off. What happened to it, and does it need the Board's approval?

With thanks,
