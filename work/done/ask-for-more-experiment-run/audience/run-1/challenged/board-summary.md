# Annual Report 2025: Board Summary

**Board meeting, 19 February 2026.** The item has 10 minutes. Reading time is about 4 minutes, or 30 seconds for "In brief".

## In brief

- **2025:** loans fell 4.7%, mostly for temporary reasons. Membership reached a record. The year ended with a deficit of 4,044 cr.
- **You decide:** there are 7 decisions. The main one is the **2026 budget**, and the budget **depends on the adult fee rise**.
- **Four need discussion:** decisions 1, 2, 3 and 6.
- **Three can be taken together:** decisions 4, 5 and 7.
- **Management recommends approving all seven.**
- **The figures are draft and not yet audited.** The audited accounts come to the Board in April.

---

## What changed

**Loans fell 4.7%**, from 101,250 to 96,480.

Most of the causes are temporary:

- **Home delivery was cut** while its post was vacant. That cost about 1,940 loans.
- **The Library was open 112 fewer hours** (the roof closure and no Thursday evenings). That cost about 1,500 to 2,000 loans.
- **Reservation waits got longer**, at 18.4 days against 16.9.

One cause is not temporary:

- **Adult borrowing is falling.** One adult member in three paid the fee but borrowed nothing.

There was also good news:

- **Membership is at a record 8,670**, up 3.1%.
- **Children's loans are at a record**, up 10.6%.

**Late returns rose** from 10.2% to 11.7%.

**Since November** (with full staff back and late opening restored), loans are only 1.0 to 2.2% below 2024.

---

## What it cost

- **Spent:** 833,700 cr
- **Received:** 829,656 cr
- **Deficit:** 4,044 cr, met from reserves

The roof repair cost 38,500 cr, of which the Town Council paid 30,000 cr. **Without the roof, the year would have shown a surplus of 4,456 cr.**

**Reserves:** 122,256 cr. That is 21,131 cr above the policy minimum.

---

## What you must decide

Decisions are numbered as in the report (§10.5).

### Decisions 1 and 2: Budget and fee (take together)

**Decision 1** is to approve the 2026 budget (report Table 29):

- **Spend:** 836,700 cr
- **Income:** 825,000 cr
- **Deficit:** 11,700 cr from reserves

The budget restores full staffing, home delivery and Thursday evenings.

What changes from 2025:

- **Staff:** up 41,100 cr, for full staffing and a 3% pay award.
- **Roof:** down 38,500 cr, because there is no repair this year.
- **IT:** down 9,300 cr, because the kiosk is already paid for.

Assumptions to watch:

- **The Council grant of 652,800 cr** has been indicated by the Council but not confirmed.
- **Fee income** assumes that up to 60 adult members leave because of the rise.
- **Management will not propose another deficit budget** for 2027. Options come to the Board in October 2026.

**Decision 2** is to raise the adult fee from 20 cr to 22 cr for memberships joined or renewed on or after 1 April 2026. Other fees stay the same. This would be the first rise since 2021.

**At stake:** the fee rise is worth about 10,600 cr a year.

- **With the rise:** reserves end 2026 about 6,000 cr above the minimum. That margin is thin: one unbudgeted repair would use it up.
- **Without the rise:** reserves end 4,632 cr **below** the minimum. Management would cut the book fund to close the gap.

### Decision 3: Keep junior borrowing fine-free

This would make the 2025 trial permanent.

- **For:** 297 children whose cards had been blocked borrowed again, and junior loans rose 8.4%.
- **Against:** about 580 extra late returns, most under a week late.

**At stake:** about 1,000 cr a year in fines forgone. Management recommends keeping the policy, with text-message reminders to parents.

### Decision 6: Roof

This would authorise the Director and the Chair to negotiate with the Council on who pays for structural roof repairs, and to prepare a bid to the Council's 2027–28 capital programme. Both come back to the Board before anything is agreed.

**At stake:** nothing is committed now. But the rest of the roof is likely to need replacing within 3 to 5 years, at about **145,000 cr**. That is more than total reserves.

### Decisions 4, 5 and 7: Routine (take together)

- **Decision 4:** turn the vacant 0.6 FTE post into an Outreach and Home Delivery Assistant, and authorise recruitment. **At stake:** nothing, because it is within the budget.
- **Decision 5:** write off 174 unpaid replacement charges from 2025. **At stake:** 2,784 cr, already provided for, so the result for the year does not change.
- **Decision 7:** approve the 2026 targets (report Table 31). The main ones are 99,000 loans, 72% of members borrowing, and a late return rate of 10.5% or less. Progress would be reported at each Board meeting. **At stake:** nothing.
