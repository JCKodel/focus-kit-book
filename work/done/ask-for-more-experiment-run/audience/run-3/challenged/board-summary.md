**Decisions 1 and 2: budget and adult fee** (§10.2–10.3)

Approve:
- Spending of 836,700 cr
- Income of 825,000 cr
- A deficit of 11,700 cr, taken from reserves

The budget relies on raising the adult fee from 20 cr to 22 cr from 1 April. This would be the first rise since 2021.

- **Both approved:** reserves end 2026 at 5,968 cr above the minimum.
- **Budget approved without the fee:** reserves end 4,632 cr below the minimum. Management would propose cutting the book fund to cover the gap.

The fee in context:
- Prices have risen about 19% since 2021, against a fee rise of 10%.
- The other two subscription libraries in the partnership charge 24 cr and 26 cr.
- The budget already allows for up to 60 adults not renewing because of the rise.
- The Friends' hardship fund will keep paying the fees of members on low incomes.

## What changed in 2025 (§2)

- **Loans** fell 4.7% to 96,480. This is the first fall in four years.
- **Membership** reached a record 8,670. But only 71% of members borrowed anything, down from 74%.
- **Main causes:**
  - home delivery was cut back after a staff vacancy
  - the roof failure closed the library for 8 days
  - the Thursday late opening was dropped for 22 weeks
  - book buying was paused while the roof cost was uncertain
- **The 2026 budget reverses all of these.** The drop in adult borrowing is only partly explained by them.
- **Children's loans** reached a record, up 11%.
- **Late returns** rose to 11.7% of loans, from 10.2%.

---

## What it cost (§9)

- **Deficit:** 4,044 cr, taken from reserves. Spending was 833,700 cr and income 829,656 cr.
- **Roof repair:** 38,500 cr, of which the Council paid 30,000 cr.
- **Staff:** 17,700 cr under budget, because posts were vacant.
- **Cost per loan:** 8.64 cr, up from 7.66 cr.
- **Reserves:** 122,256 cr, which is 21,131 cr above the minimum.

## Worth asking

- Is a margin of 5,968 cr above the minimum enough, with the roof ahead? Management's answer: the 2026 budget has no one-off works, and a 3,500 cr roof survey in the spring will size the roof costs before the 2027 budget is set.
- How confident is management of 99,000 loans? The report says "achievable but not certain."
