# 2026-04-08 — overdue members & suspension (who does what)

Notes: RL

## Context

Suspension rules from February (Feb 11 notes) say a member is suspended once a loan is 28 days overdue. What we never pinned down: *who* actually suspends, and what an overdue member can do in the meantime. Rafe needs both before writing any suspension code.

## How Wren does it today

- Once a week (usually Monday morning) Wren goes through the "who has what" sheet and highlights anything long overdue.
- Then looks people up, checks the card, sometimes rings them first.
- Only after that does the member get a "suspended" note on their card.
- Wren: "Half the time there's a reason. Someone's in hospital, the book was returned to the wrong branch, the sheet's just wrong."

So it's a person making the call, with the sheet as input. Not a hard switch.

## Discussion

- Rafe had assumed the system would suspend automatically the moment the threshold is crossed. Odile and Wren both didn't like that. Odile: "I don't want a member turned away at the desk by a computer nobody can argue with."
- Tamsin suggested a middle ground: the model can *work out* who should be suspended, but a librarian runs it and is recorded as the one who did it. Wren liked that — it's basically the Monday routine, minus the highlighter.
- Bex asked what happens between "overdue" and "suspended". E.g. member is 10 days overdue on one book, comes in to borrow another.
  - Wren: today they'd just lend it. Maybe a word at the desk.
  - Odile: agreed. Overdue alone isn't a reason to refuse; the suspension is the point where we stop.
  - Juno checked: so a member with an overdue loan can still check out new items, as long as they're under the loan limit and not suspended? Yes.
- Marek (late) asked if suspension should be automatic "eventually", e.g. a nightly job. Parked. Nothing nightly exists and we're not building infrastructure.
- Reinstating stays as per Jan 26 for now — only Odile. Wren said that's a bit of a bottleneck when Odile's on leave, but didn't want to reopen it today.

## Decisions

1. **Librarians suspend, not the system by itself.** The model provides a function that finds members over the threshold and suspends them, but it's run by a librarian and records which librarian did it, the date and a reason.
2. **Members with overdue loans may keep borrowing until they are suspended.** Checkout only refuses suspended members (and the usual loan limit / copy checks). No separate "has overdue" block.
3. Every suspension needs a reason — no blank reasons. (`reason-required`)
4. Threshold unchanged for now (28 days, per Feb 11).

## Not decided

- Whether 28 days is right. Wren thinks it's too long; Odile wants to see how it goes first.
- Reinstatement — who and when. Leaving as is.
