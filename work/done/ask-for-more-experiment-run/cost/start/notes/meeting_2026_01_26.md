## Book vs copy

- What we called "book" is really two things.
  - **Book** = the title. Title, author, sometimes an ISBN (older stuff and local-history pamphlets often have none).
  - **Copy** = the physical item on the shelf. This is what gets lent.
- Saltgate has e.g. 4 copies of the same popular crime novel. Members borrow a copy, not a book.
- Each copy has a barcode sticker: `C-0001`, `C-0002`, ... Wren says numbering is sequential across the branch, not per title. Some old ones are handwritten.
- -> loans reference a **copy**, not a book. RL to update the design sketch.

## Copy statuses

Odile + Wren listed what a copy can be:

- available
- on-loan
- lost — member says they lost it, or it's just never come back
- damaged — coffee, dog, bath (Wren: "always the bath")
- withdrawn — taken out of circulation on purpose (old, superseded edition, etc.)

So 5 statuses. Lost and damaged copies stay on the catalogue sheet with a mark; Odile likes being able to see how many copies of a title went missing.
