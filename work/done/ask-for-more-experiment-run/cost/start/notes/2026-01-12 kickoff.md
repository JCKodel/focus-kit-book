## Why we're here

CardBox, per Odile: a card index plus three drifted-apart spreadsheets (catalogue, members, "who has what"). Loans go on paper cards at the desk, (usually) typed into the third sheet at day's end. Odile: "The cards are the truth, the spreadsheets are a rumour."

Juno: we're not replacing CardBox in one go. First, a clean **domain model** of lending, with tests, that team and library staff both understand. UI and storage come later, if at all.

## Goals (as agreed)

1. Model books, members and loans in code, with the real rules as tests.
2. Get the rules out of Odile's head (and Wren's, and the card margin notes) into one place.
3. Keep it small. TypeScript, Node, no frameworks we don't need.
4. Odile can read the test names and say "yes, that's how it works".

## Out of scope (for now)

- Moving data out of CardBox
- Any member-facing screens
- Reservations / holds ("we don't really do those, people just ask at the desk")
- Email reminders
- Inter-branch transfers

Juno: "for now" means for now, not forever. Odile agreed.

## Current rules (as described by Odile today)

- **Loan period: 14 days.** Stamped on the card at checkout.
- **Max 3 loans per member** at a time. Odile: "Three is plenty. People hoard."
- **Renewals: unlimited.** At the desk (or by phone), librarian re-stamps. No cap.
- **Fines: likely 0.20 per day overdue.** Council "is leaning towards" bringing them back, per item. Not confirmed; Odile wants us to plan for it. Juno: "assume fines exist, keep the amount configurable."
- **Overdue members:** eventually "we stop lending to them"; Wren (handles most of it at the desk) to describe practice.
- **Lost/damaged books:** a margin note in the catalogue sheet today. Needs proper discussion.

Marek: "14 days" from checkout day, or "two weeks, same weekday"? Odile: same thing? Marek: mostly. Parked; Marek to think about dates generally.

## Discussion bits

- Scale: ~1,900 active members across Hollowmere, ~11,000 items at Saltgate. Performance is not a design driver.
- Users: librarians at the desk, eventually; members never touch it.
- Tamsin: tests from day one, no external dependencies if avoidable; Node's built-in test runner. No objections.
- Spreadsheets not needed yet; Odile will send column headers for vocabulary.
- Children's cards: same rules, just a different colour card.

## Decisions

- Domain model only. No UI, no persistence beyond what tests need.
- TypeScript on Node; tests with `node:test`.
- Today's rules as initial numbers (14 days / 3 loans / unlimited renewals / fines likely 0.20/day).
- Weekly-ish check-in with Odile; Juno brokers in between.

## Open questions

- Fines — confirmed or not? Who decides? (Council, apparently.)
- Who may do what — any librarian, or only Odile for some things?
