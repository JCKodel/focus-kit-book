## Loans

1. **Suspension: after how long overdue does a member get "stopped"?**
   — Odile: 4 weeks. "If it's a month late, they're stopped." (11 Feb)

2. Does *any* overdue item stop a member borrowing more, or only once they're suspended?
   — Odile: only once they're suspended. Until then the desk just reminds them. "We don't want to turn people away over two days." (11 Feb)

3. Is the loan period the same for every kind of item (DVDs, local history, kids' books)?
   — Odile: yes, all the same. (11 Feb)

4. Fines: has the council decided yet?
   — Odile: not yet. Still "probably 0.20". (11 Feb)

5. Who decides that someone gets suspended? Automatic or a librarian?
   — Odile: a librarian does it, but they should be told who's due for it. Record who did it and why. (11 Feb)

## Books
