## 1. Suspensions

Odile's view: today in CardBox nobody is ever really suspended, the desk just "has a word" with people. That works for regulars and fails for everyone else. Odile wants a clear rule the system enforces so staff don't have to be the bad guy.

- Asked when someone should be suspended. Odile: **"four weeks"** overdue on any single loan. Not counting total days across loans, just the worst one.
- We'll model it as **28 days overdue** → member suspended.
- Juno asked whether it should be automatic or a librarian action. Odile: automatic flag is fine, but someone should see a list each morning. Tamsin: we can do a daily pass over loans and return who should be suspended; the "list each morning" is a UI thing for later.
- Suspended member: can't borrow, can't renew. Odile was clear on this.
- **Reinstatement: only the head librarian** (i.e. Odile) can lift a suspension. Same as Odile said in January. Odile wants to talk to the person first. Marek asked what happens when Odile is on leave — "then they wait."
- Suspension needs a reason and a date recorded. Odile wants to be able to read back why someone was suspended a year later.

## 2. Member data

Keep it minimal. Odile does not want to hold anything that isn't needed.

- **Name** and **email**. That's it for now.
- No address, no phone, no date of birth. CardBox has addresses for about half the members and most are out of date anyway.
- Email is for contacting people about overdue books; not used for anything automatic yet.
- Tamsin: email gets trimmed and lower-cased on registration, basic format check. Odile fine with that.
- Juno asked about children's cards / guardians. Parked, Odile says it's rare at Saltgate.
- Member id: generated by us, not the old CardBox card number. Odile doesn't mind.

## 3. Returns

- **Returns are always accepted.** Overdue, suspended, doesn't matter — Odile: "I never want someone turned away at the desk with a book in their hand."
- Marek: so `returnCopy` never checks suspension. Agreed.
- Book returned → copy available again straight away.

## 4. AOB

- Odile mentioned people waiting for popular books and the paper list Wren keeps. Juno and Marek to write up a proposal.
- Fines came up briefly again. Odile less keen than in January; will think about it. Not blocking anything.
- Next meeting with the library early March, Wren to join.

## Decisions
- Suspension after **28 days** overdue on any loan.
- Only the head librarian reinstates.
- Members: name + email only.
- Returns always accepted, never blocked.
