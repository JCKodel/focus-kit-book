# ch11-queue-grows

Draft, from the author's reading of the book on 2026-10-18.

* Chapter 11, "Tamanho e ordem" (pt 11:73, key point 11:174), reads as if three to eight deliveries were a limit. It is only how a milestone starts.
* Each delivery or review can add lines; users' tests find problems that, by talking to the agent, become more lines, even intermediate milestones. The queue is a living, dynamic document; the team decides what enters and its priority. A milestone of 5 lines ending with 50 is not unusual.
* Agents tend to add. Without human judgment a milestone can grow without control; a line being added may belong to a future milestone of improvements, not to now.
* Chapters 9, 10 and 23 say the first milestone starts with three to eight; check they read as a start, not a limit.
* A suggestion to the kit (a mark for "if there is time") is in work/kit-proposals.md; the book teaches the kit as it is (book-is-the-kit-today).
